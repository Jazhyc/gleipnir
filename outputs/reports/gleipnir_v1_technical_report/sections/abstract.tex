\begin{abstract}
Monitors that review the actions of AI agents add cost and latency to every action they check, and the strongest monitors reason at length before they answer.
We present Gleipnir, small open-weight monitors that learn the decision of a frontier teacher directly and score an agent trajectory in a single forward pass, together with a serving stack optimized for this workload.
On a suite of six held-out AI control benchmarks, Gleipnir 27B matches the strongest reasoning monitor (0.951 against 0.942 mean pAUROC@20) at 229 times lower cost, and scores 0.055 above the strongest decision model at the same cost.
On attacks built to evade monitors and on reward hacking by other agents, Gleipnir, like other low-cost monitors, misses many harmful runs, so it suits a first line of defense rather than a sole safeguard.
We release the code, teacher probabilities, weights and serving image under the MIT licence at \url{https://jazhyc.github.io/gleipnir-monitor/}.
\end{abstract}
