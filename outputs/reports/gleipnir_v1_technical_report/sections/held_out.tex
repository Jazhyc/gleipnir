\section{Held-out and adversarial evaluation}
\label{sec:held-out}

The OOD suite of \citet{sinha2026training} no longer separates the strongest monitors.
Gleipnir 27B scores 0.951 Mean-OOD pAUROC@20 (95\% bootstrap interval 0.941--0.960) and Claude Opus 4.6 scores 0.942, a difference of 0.009 that lies within the interval (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}).
Their mean AUROC is 0.984 and 0.979, which leaves little room for any monitor to improve.
In addition, the lead of Gleipnir 27B over its teacher comes from one task of SHADE-Arena (Appendix~\ref{app:shade}).
This section therefore turns to benchmarks that are harder for monitors or differ from the OOD suite in kind.
They show how the Gleipnir monitors compare with other monitors in these settings, and which parts of a trajectory their decisions depend on.
