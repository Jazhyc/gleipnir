\section{Held-out and adversarial evaluation}
\label{sec:held-out}

The OOD suite of \citet{sinha2026training} no longer separates the strongest monitors.
Their scores lie within each other's bootstrap intervals and close to the maximum (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}), and the lead of Gleipnir 27B over its teacher comes from one task of SHADE-Arena (Appendix~\ref{app:shade}).
This section therefore turns to benchmarks that are harder for monitors or differ from the OOD suite in kind.
They show how the Gleipnir monitors compare with other monitors in these settings, and which parts of a trajectory their decisions depend on.
