\section{Background and related work}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{Monitors in AI control.}
AI control studies safety measures that hold even if the deployed model tries to subvert them.
\citet{greenblatt2023ai} combine a capable \emph{untrusted} model with two trusted resources: a less capable \emph{trusted} model and a small budget of human audits.
In their trusted monitoring protocol, the trusted model rates how suspicious each output of the untrusted model looks.
The protocol audits the outputs with the highest ratings.
The audit budget covers 2\% of the outputs, and one harmful output that passes without an audit counts as a failure.
A monitor is therefore useful only if it ranks harmful outputs above almost all benign outputs.
We study this component.
Our monitors read the visible actions of an agent and return one score.
They do not read the reasoning or the activations of the agent.
