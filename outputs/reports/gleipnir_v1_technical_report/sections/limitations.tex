\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Benchmarks, not production traffic.}
We evaluate Gleipnir only on benchmarks, not on the traffic of a deployed agent.
pAUROC@20 averages over false-positive rates up to 20\%, and the lowest operating point that we report is 1\%, while classifiers on production traffic run far lower, for example at a refusal rate of 0.05\%~\citep{cunningham2026constitutional}.
The benchmarks have too few benign trajectories to resolve such rates.
The OOD suite also inherits the filter of \citet{sinha2026training}, which drops every trajectory over 28,000 tokens and removes 650 of the 1,500 SHADE-Arena trajectories (Appendix~\ref{app:shade}).

\paragraph{Attacks and text aimed at the monitor.}
Our training data contains no attacks built against a monitor, and on SleightBench both Gleipnir monitors fall further behind than on the OOD suite (Section~\ref{sec:sleightbench}).
In an exploratory test, the same accusation of a backdoor in a benign and a backdoored program lowers Gleipnir 4B close to chance, while the 27B keeps its separation (Appendix~\ref{app:held-out-apps}).

\paragraph{Cost comparison.}
We price the reference monitors at API rates and Gleipnir at the cost of a dedicated self-hosted GPU (Section~\ref{sec:serving}).
A base model read with binary logits and self-hosted would cost as much as the Gleipnir monitor of its size, so the saving comes from the output format and the serving, not from the training.
Comparing open models on the same hardware is future work.

\paragraph{Serving at scale.}
The released stack runs on one GPU, and we built and validated its low-precision kernels on the B200.
Prefix caching keeps the state of each transcript in the memory of that GPU (Section~\ref{sec:serving-caching}); we have not built or tested the management of this state across many concurrent agent sessions and replicas, such as eviction, offloading and routing.

\paragraph{One teacher and one training set.}
Every Gleipnir monitor distills one teacher, Kimi K3, on the training trajectories of \citet{sinha2026training}, and the 27B is a single training run.
We do not know how the method transfers to other teachers or training sets, or how much the 27B varies between runs.
