\section{Team}
\label{sec:team}
I am the sole investigator, working full time. I led Gleipnir v1
\citep{ferrao2026gleipnir}: I built its teacher data, training method and
serving stack, ran its main evaluations and released its code, weights, data
and serving image. v1 was supported by a BlueDot Impact Rapid Grant and the
exploration phase of Neel Nanda's MATS stream.

The project is fast-paced and planned for one person, building on the v1
code. The end date is fixed because I start the MATS main phase in
mid-January 2027, when collaborating outside the program may be difficult. If
others join, they would mainly run additional evaluations.
