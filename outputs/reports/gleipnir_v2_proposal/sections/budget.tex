\section{Budget}
\label{sec:budget}
GPU time is priced at \$8 per hour for one NVIDIA B200, the current on-demand
rate at Runpod. The GPU-hours are educated guesses from the experience of v1;
Section~\ref{sec:risks} discusses where they may run over, and the contingency
covers that. API credits pay for baseline monitors on production traffic, so
that the new thresholds can be compared with other monitors; on the six
benchmarks, the baseline scores of v1 are reused.

M4's GPU time covers several monitors (the v1 monitors, their pruned versions
and Gleipnir on Inkling 2 Mini) on SWE-chat and other datasets, including
sessions longer than the v1 context. Its API credits cover inexpensive
decision models and monitors on the full traffic.
