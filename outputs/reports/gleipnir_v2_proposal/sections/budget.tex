\section{Budget}
\label{sec:budget}
GPU time is priced at \$8 per hour for one NVIDIA B200, the current on-demand
rate at Runpod. The estimates follow the measured costs of v1: training
Gleipnir 27B once takes 4.2 GPU-hours, the first screen of Inkling 2 Mini took
about 3, and one pass over the 16,625 SWE-chat sessions that fit in the v1
context takes 2.2 for Gleipnir 4B and 6.7 for Gleipnir 27B. No teacher is
queried. API credits pay for baseline monitors on production traffic, so that
the new thresholds can be compared with other monitors; on the six benchmarks,
the baseline scores of v1 are reused.

M4's GPU time covers several monitors (the v1 monitors, their pruned versions
and Gleipnir on Inkling 2 Mini) on SWE-chat and other datasets, including
sessions longer than the v1 context. Its API credits cover inexpensive
decision models and monitors on the full traffic; a frontier reasoning monitor
would cost thousands of dollars per pass at this volume, so it is run at most
on a sample.
