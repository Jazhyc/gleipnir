\section{Plan and milestones}
\label{sec:plan}
The project runs for about six and a half weeks, from 1 December 2026 to
mid-January 2027, with the aims in order. The two prefill aims are short and
reach the released v1 monitors within the first two weeks. Gleipnir on
Inkling 2 Mini takes most of the time, about three and a half weeks, and
starts after its software support has had two more weeks to mature. The
operating points are quick to compute; their main cost is GPU time. Each
evaluation, including the held-out selection rule and the pass mark, is fixed
before the candidates are scored.
