\section{Plan and milestones}
\label{sec:plan}
The project runs for about six and a half weeks, from 1 December 2026 to
mid-January 2027, with the aims in order. The two prefill aims are short and
reach the released v1 monitors within the first two and a half weeks. Gleipnir
on Inkling 2 Mini takes most of the time, about three and a half weeks, and
starts after its software support has had that long to mature. The operating points are quick
to compute; their main cost is API credits for the baseline monitors. Each milestone releases its code,
weights and results when it is reached, and a short report closes the
project. The success criterion and evaluation of each aim are fixed before any
candidate is scored.
