## Scheduling controls

The [0.31 release](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/releases/tag/v0.31.0)
adds independent active admission and adaptive long-prefill thresholds. Three
tests against the real scheduler confirm:

- Uncorrected pooling still stalls at 32,767 computed tokens for an exact-32K
  request. Carry the source-bound zero-generated-token reservation correction.
- With two eligible requests and budget 1,024, adaptive threshold 128 allocates
  512 each. A lone long request uses all 1,024 tokens.
- Active admission can be capped independently of runner capacity.

Compare stock FCFS, adaptive 4K chunking and stock FCFS with active admission 16.
Keep runner capacity 128 and budget 32,768 in all conditions. Select 16 from the
archived concurrency plateau before observing these timings. Adaptive chunking
can change FP4 producer paths and accumulation boundaries; these are whole-stack
scheduling comparisons. Async generation/speculation and the reworked KV-holder
waiting queue are not separately measured on this prefix-cache-free pooling task.
