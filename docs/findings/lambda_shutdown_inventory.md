# Lambda shutdown artifact inventory

This record captures the artifacts retained locally before shutting down the
ephemeral `monitor-foundation` Lambda instance. The inventory was verified on
2026-08-18. Generated archives, predictions, datasets, and logs are intentionally
ignored by Git; the hashes below identify the local copies.

## Retained model artifacts

`results/shutdown-selected-masters.tar` contains 12 top-level causal-LM adapter
masters and no intermediate checkpoints. Each master was checked to contain 256
FP32 tensors. The archive retains three seeds for each model family needed to
reproduce the headline comparisons:

- internal/AISI soft-only rank-128 adapters;
- internal/AISI hard-only rank-128 adapters;
- internal/AISI soft plus `0.50` hard-label rank-128 adapters; and
- the best mean-AUROC full-data adapter-capacity point, rank 128.

The causal masters are the durable artifacts. Derived serving adapters were not
duplicated because they can be regenerated by the recorded checksum-rebasing
procedure and require matched parity checks before use.

## Deliberately not retained

The remote result tree was about 447 GB, dominated by duplicate adapters and
intermediate checkpoints from completed data/rank grids and optimizer,
regularization, and training-procedure screens. Those weights are not model
candidates: their final metrics, summaries, launch configurations, source
revisions, and conclusions are already local or documented, and they can be
reproduced from the committed experiment code. Keeping them would add hundreds
of gigabytes without preserving additional poster evidence.

Immediately before shutdown authorization, `nvidia-smi` reported no active GPU
compute processes. On 2026-08-18, instance
`6c28855cece146fa989a9aedfccc6573` entered `terminating` after the authorized
termination request. A subsequent Lambda API query returned no matching active
instances, confirming that the two-H100 capacity was released.
